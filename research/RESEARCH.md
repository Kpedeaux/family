# Pedeaux Family — Research Log

The working record behind **family.kevinpedeaux.com**. Every fact on the site traces to an entry
here; every entry here cites its record. The site (`assets/js/data.js` and the two HTML pages) is
the presentation; this file is the evidence locker.

Started 2026-08-08. The paternal line broke open 2026-08-09 via the Diocese of Baton Rouge
sacramental abstracts. The maternal line broke open 2026-08-10 via Judy Larmann Gifford's family
papers, verified same day against the Louisiana state indexes.

**Confidence tags:** **[VERIFIED]** = an official civil or sacramental record names both people in
the link · **[CONFIRM]** = likely but single-sourced (family record, user memorial, compiled tree) ·
**[INFERRED]** = reasoned from circumstance; no document names both people · **[TODO]** = open.

**The method rule:** never record a parent-child link without a record naming *both* people.
A shared surname is not evidence.

**Source hierarchy:** Catholic sacramental register abstracts and Louisiana civil vital records
(best) → federal census → family records (Esther's ledger, Judy's notes, tomb rosters) → published
obituary → Find a Grave (user-submitted, weakest).

**Register codes:** ASM = Assumption / Plattenville · ASC = Ascension · SPH = St. Philomena,
Labadieville · SMI = St. Michael, Convent (St. James Parish) · SJA = St. James · SGA = St. Gabriel ·
PCP = Pointe Coupée.

## The Acadian line (Cormier, acadiansingray.com — [CONFIRM], not verified)

- Michel Aucoin *le jeune*, b. c.1732 probably Cobeguit; s/o Antoine Aucoin le jeune (c.1700–1756)
  × Élisabeth Amireau (c.1709–1766). To Île Saint-Jean 1755/56; married Isabelle Hébert there
  c.1758; deported, landed Saint-Malo 23 Jan 1759 (Rieder, *The Acadians in France*, vol. 3, port
  of Saint-Servan, p. 21, family #1). Settled Saint-Énogat; fourteen children.
- **1785:** La Ville d'Archangel, 600-ton frigate, left Saint-Malo 12 Aug, reached New Orleans
  3 Dec 1785; 309 aboard; sixth of the Seven Ships. Passenger entry 21 names all twelve, incl.
  "Floriane, 4 years."
- First to Bayou des Écores, where Michel died by 1793; widow and children then to upper Bayou
  Lafourche. Cormier: "Younger daughters Isabelle and Florianne Marguerite married into the Potier,
  Pedeau, and Barrilleaux families on the Lafourche."
- Florence was also called **"Clémence"** — useful search alias.
- Full Saint-Énogat transcription: Albert J. Robichaux, Jr., *The Acadian Exiles in Saint-Malo,
  1758–1785*, vol. 1, pp. 22–24, family #31.
- ⚠️ **Cormier is fallible:** his narrative gives Florence's second husband as "François Allain le
  jeune"; the register says **Bariot** (see Vol. 3 above). His own index line agrees with the
  register. Treat his narrative as a lead, not proof.
- Cormier has **no Pedeau entry at all** (checked including Book Eleven's "Foreign French" index:
  Patin → Pelletier → Penisson, no Pedeau). Valuable for the Aucoin side only.
- Possible Italian thread: Cormier derives the Louisiana **Cancienne** family from "Pietro, son of
  Giorgio Cancieni and Margherita Catharina Yearne of Venice, Italy." If Marie Cancienne belongs to
  that family (not proven for her), there is an Italian line two centuries older than the Brunettis.

**Saint-Énogat cote formula** (AD35, image-only, session-bound viewer): Baptêmes/Mariages,

# THE GAIENNIE LINE — details

- **Edna's birth record doesn't exist** because she was born at Thibodaux (8 Sep 1894) and Louisiana
  didn't require registration outside Orleans until 1918. Confirmed by pattern: siblings born
  1893–1905 (Lucile Marie 1896 · infant d. Jun 1897 · Lawrence Herbert Nov 1897 · George Florestan
  1905) are ALL absent from the Orleans index; those born before and after are all present.
  Her siblings in the index (father GAIENNIE ALPHONSE F / mother CURIEN JEANNE): Charles
  (13 Jan 1889) · Corinne (14 Nov 1890, m. George Harris Jessup 1915) · Edward J (14 Mar 1892,
  d. 2 Jun 1894) · Roland (21 Feb 1909).
- **Alphonse Jr. and Jeanne Curien died ten weeks apart in the winter of 1919** — very likely the
  influenza pandemic. 🔵 Marriage Feb 1888 Orleans Vol. 13 p. 255 · his death 19 Mar 1919 Vol. 176
  p. 115 · hers 12 Jan 1919 Vol. 175 p. 218. [VERIFIED]
- **The A. F. Gaiennie Tomb, Greenwood Cemetery** ("5 Palmetto Magnolia Hawthorne") is Alphonse
  Jr.'s own tomb; Edna brought her whole Pedeaux household into it: Alphonse Jr. · Jeanne · Edward J
  · the 1897 infant · Lawrence Herbert · William Lytle Gaiennie (1960–1993) · Edna · Numa Sr. ·
  Robert Louis · Lorraine Marie · Numa Jr. No other Gaiennie branch is in it. Kevin confirms it as
  a known family site.
- **Marie Alix Fazende** (6 Jun 1841 – 23 May 1877), d/o Sébastien François Angèl Morière Fazende
  (1795–1876) × Marie Félicité Estelle Verloin deGruy (1820–1883) — hence the deGruy family site's
  Gaiennie pages. ⚠️ NOPL obituary index lists her as "Mrs. Marie Alix **Moriere**" — search both.
- **War of 1812:** compiled militia rosters carry "Gervais Gainne — 6 Reg't. (Landry's)" and
  "Francis Gaignie — 4 Reg't. (Morgan's)" plus a "U. Gaignie" — seven Gaiennie entries across
