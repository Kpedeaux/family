### Alfred Moizant, 1900 and 1910
Search: https://www.familysearch.org/search/record/results?q.surname=Moizant&q.residencePlace=Plaquemines%2C+Louisiana

Index returns:
- **`Alfred Moizant · Census 1900 · Wards 5 & 10, Plaquemines · Birth January 1838 France ·
  Marriage 1866 · Children Amelie M., Angeline, Clemantine J., Louise, Adeline, Anitia`**
- **`Alfred L Moizant · Census 1910 · Nairn, Plaquemines · Birth 1838 France · Spouses
  Victoria · Children Angeline, Adelina`**

Site text: "1900 — Wards 5 and 10, Plaquemines Parish: Alfred Moizant, b. January 1838 in
France, married 1866, with Amelie, Angeline, Clemantine, Louise, Adeline and Anitia at
home. · 1910 — NAIRN, Plaquemines Parish." → **CONFIRMED exactly** — birth month and year,
birthplace, marriage year, and all six daughters by name.

**And it confirms the site's best Moizant observation.** The page argues that in 1910
Alfred was living at Nairn, "the same small delta village as his son-in-law Tony Franovich,
in the same census year." Both men return Nairn, 1910, from the index independently. That
was an inference drawn by reading two records side by side; it is now demonstrated.

### Clémence Chapeau — via the daughter named for her
Index: **`Clemantine J. Moizant · Census 1900 · Wards 5 & 10, Plaquemines · Birth December
1876 · Parents Victoria Moizant, Alfred Moizant`**
Site text: "In Alfred Moizant's household: 'Clemantine J. Moizant.'" → **CONFIRMED.**
The site uses this to argue the daughter was named for her Bordeaux grandmother Clémence
Chapeau. The census entry exists exactly as quoted; the naming inference remains an
inference, and is presented as one.

**Bonus:** `Victoria Moizant · Birth January 1850 Louisiana · Marriage 1866` corroborates
the 9 January 1850 birth this site carries for Victoria Daroca, to the month.
